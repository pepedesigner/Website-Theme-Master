Once your quota is spent, tencent/hy3-preview takes over for free at 3 requests per minute, capped at 60 per day, if you had paid usage this month. It is not covered by the SLA.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why isn't there a $1 plan?

After fixed payment fees, a $1 plan earns almost nothing and mostly attracts bulk-created accounts. We use a $4.9 first month plus a $5 trial instead, which filters abuse and still costs less than a coffee.

Does the daily burst add to my monthly quota?

No. A burst raises the ceiling of the current 6h window only — $15 to $20 on Standard. Everything you spend still counts against the monthly pool, which the burst does not raise.

Can unused quota roll over?

No. Quota is monthly and resets at the end of the cycle. Rollover would make our compute cost unpredictable, which is exactly what the flat price depends on.

Why 402 and not 429 when I run out?

Clients like Claude Code retry automatically on 429. Using 429 for "out of quota" would trigger a retry storm and burn compute. 402 tells the client to stop and ask you to top up or wait.

Can I use one subscription across several clients?

Yes. The quota pool and the five-request concurrency limit are shared across every client on the account — Cursor, Claude Code and OpenCode at the same time.

What if SandCode goes down?

We commit to 99.9% API availability in the first year. If our routing layer fails for more than 30 consecutive minutes, affected users can claim a $2 PAYG credit and have the downtime prorated off the bill.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — if you have made no requests within 7 days, the cancellation is refunded automatically. Disputes on multi-account abuse are handled with GitHub and device evidence.

How does the $5 invite reward work?

When someone you invite subscribes and completes their first paid month, you both receive $5 in PAYG credit. It is capped at 10 effective invites per month and $50 lifetime per account, and the credit expires with the month it was issued.

Do rewards count against my monthly quota?

No. Rewards are PAYG wallet balance. They are spent like a top-up and never touch the monthly quota pool.
