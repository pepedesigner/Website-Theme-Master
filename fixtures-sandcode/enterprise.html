Controls

Security that ships with the agent.

No training on your code

Prompts and outputs are never used to train a model — ours or an upstream provider's. That commitment is written into the DPA, not just the marketing page.

Tiered retention

Billing metadata is kept 24 months. Tool-call records are kept 90 days and can be switched off. Prompt content is not stored unless you opt in, and then it is redacted with a 7-day lifetime.

Secret redaction

Requests are pattern-matched in flight and plaintext AWS, GitHub and Stripe keys are stripped before anything is processed or logged.

Dependency guardrails

Model output is matched against live package-index data, so a hallucinated dependency is flagged in the response rather than installed.

Boundaries

Where your data lives.

Data residency

Choose the region your data is stored in, and exclude specific regions entirely. EU and US options are available, with residency terms attached to the DPA.

One-click deletion

Anything you opted into can be deleted in one action, and the deletion is confirmed rather than queued silently.

Availability commitment

99.9% API availability in the first year. A routing-layer outage over 30 consecutive minutes earns affected accounts a $2 PAYG credit and a prorated reduction.

Transparent scope

We do not promise 99.99%, and the free survival tier is excluded from the SLA. An honest number you can hold us to beats a rounder one.

Teams

Seats on one pool

Seats are $24.9/month each, or $19.9 on annual billing, with a 5-seat minimum. Every seat adds $175 of quota to a single shared pool, so a heavy week in one team does not leave another strand — and buying five seats gets you a sixth free.

Per-member caps

Set a daily ceiling per member — say $3/day for a typical engineer — and lift it for architects who need more.

Roles and audit

Owner, admin and member roles with an audit history of who changed what, backed by SSO and automatic provisioning.

Invoicing

VAT and business-tax invoices generated through Stripe Tax, downloadable from the Console with your company identifiers attached.
