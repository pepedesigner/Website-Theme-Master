FAQ




What is SandCode?
Flat-rate coding compute for agent clients. $9.9/mo buys a $70 model pool, bundled search and scrape, and a local verification harness — usable from any client that accepts a custom base URL.


How do I get started?
Install with curl -fsSL https://sandcode.ai/install | bash, then run sandbase setup. It detects your clients, links your account and injects the endpoints and MCP tools in one pass.


Does the daily burst raise my monthly quota?
No. It only raises the ceiling of the current 6h window — $15 to $20 on Standard. Spend still counts against the monthly pool, which the burst does not raise.


Can unused quota roll over to next month?
No. Quota is monthly and resets at the end of the cycle. Rollover would make compute cost unpredictable, and the flat price depends on it staying predictable.


Why does running out return 402 and not 429?
Clients retry automatically on 429. Using it for "out of quota" would cause a retry storm and burn compute. 402 stops the client and tells you to top up or wait.


Does the local harness read my code?
It runs on your machine and reports only token usage metadata back. It is installed with explicit consent, and sandbase harness off turns it off at any time.


Is there a referral reward?
Yes. When someone you invite subscribes and completes their first paid month, you both get $5 in PAYG credit — capped at 10 effective invites a month and $50 lifetime per account.


Do rewards eat into my monthly quota?
No. Rewards are PAYG wallet balance, spent like a top-up. They never touch the monthly quota pool.
