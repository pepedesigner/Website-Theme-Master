How keys are bound

  • Each key is tied to your account, a device fingerprint and the 5-request concurrency limit
  • Rotate a key from the CLI, or revoke it here instantly
  • Usage from an unusual region or too many devices triggers re-verification
  • Config written by setup uses short-lived tokens, never a long-lived secret
